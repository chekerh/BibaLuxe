# Design Inspiration & UX Notes

## Ecommerce Benchmarks

- **Nike / Seahawk Media case study** – Hero sections with large visuals + CTA drive focus on new launches ([seahawkmedia.com](https://seahawkmedia.com/design/ecommerce-ui-design-themes-and-example/?utm_source=openai)).
- **Common store patterns (via David Schimke)** – Sticky navigation, card-based product grids, progressive disclosure keep browsing effortless ([davidschimke.com](https://davidschimke.com/ui-patterns-for-web-design/?utm_source=openai)).
- **UX trends (OptiMonk & CPLUZ)** – Micro-interactions, simplified checkout, optional dark mode, gesture support for mobile-first experiences ([optimonk.com](https://www.optimonk.com/ecommerce-ux-trends/?utm_source=openai), [cpluz.com](https://cpluz.com/blog/the-top-8-ui-ux-design-patterns-that-will-skyrocket-your-e-commerce-website-in-2025/?utm_source=openai)).

## 3D + Visualization Research

- **Mattress 3D marketing impact** – Omnichannel teams lean on 3D models to reduce returns and show details ([resources.imagine.io](https://resources.imagine.io/blog/why-mattress-3d-models-are-critical-for-omnichannel-marketing?utm_source=openai)).
- **Interactive UI inspiration** – Collections of 3D web UI (Dribbble, Pinterest, Easeout) showcase layered hero scenes and scroll-activated motion.

## Patterns Reflected in the Build

1. **Sticky gradient navbar** with CTA + quick actions (search/cart/account) mirrors top stores.
2. **Immersive hero** couples dual R3F scenes with layered headlines/CTAs similar to Nike & Wayfair landing pages.
3. **Segmented product rails** – separate grids for mattresses/furniture following Wayfair/Amazon category layout.
4. **Trust badges / feature bar** – shipping, warranty, satisfaction callouts derived from Amazon/Shopify best practices.
5. **Rich product cards** – price, strike-through MSRP, ratings, quick-add affordances inspired by Shopify theme gallery patterns.
6. **Scrolling story** – About + contact CTA blocks echo Section-based storytelling from high-end furniture boutiques.
